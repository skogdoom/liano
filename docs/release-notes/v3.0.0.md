# Liano 3.0.0

*Draft, prepared in M9c on `release-verification`. The owner publishes it with the
`v3.0.0` tag once the final verification is done; device results may still change it.*

Liano now runs on [Phaser](https://phaser.io/) 4 and has a new, painted look. The game
plays exactly as before: the same rules, jungle, obstacles, modes, scores and sounds.

## New

- **Cartoon graphics.** A painted jungle, monkeys, creatures, temples and menus, after the
  day and night covers. It is the new default.
- **Classic graphics** are one setting away: **C** on the title screen opens Config, and
  **Classic graphics: On/Off** (or **X** while Config is open) switches. The game reloads
  with the other look and keeps the mode and settings.
- **Rendering quality** in Config: Performance, Balanced, Sharp or Auto (keys 1, 2, 3, A).
  Both choices are remembered on the device.
- **A loading indicator** when opening the game takes a moment. It pulses instead of
  turning with reduced motion.
- **Full screen fills the screen** in single player: no sky above the jungle canopy, and
  less empty space above it in portrait.

## Smoother

- The camera eases in two smooth steps instead of one and follows three quarters of each
  swing, so the world no longer lurches at a catch.
- The background layers move more slowly, and the canopy and floor are softened slightly,
  so they no longer look stepped in fast flight.
- Frame times are evened out to the screen's refresh, which removes an unevenness Safari
  showed on 60 Hz screens.
- Sounds, shaders, the obstacle builders and the HUD's text are prepared before the title,
  to avoid hitches when something first appears.

## Good to know

- The first visit downloads more: about 0.4 MB of scripts (gzipped) and the selected art
  at the screen's resolution, 7.4–24 MB for the cartoon graphics or 1–3.9 MB for the
  classic ones. Later visits use the browser's cache.
- Graphics and quality choices are kept in the browser's storage; scores are still kept
  only until you reload.
- `?frames=1` shows a frame-time readout for checking smoothness on a device.

## For developers

- Phaser 4.2.1 replaces PixiJS 8. All art is pre-rendered into texture atlases at 1×, 2×
  and 4×: the classic pack is generated from the original drawing code, the cartoon pack
  from painted masters. Any piece can be replaced with a PNG or SVG of the same name
  ([art guide](../../art/README.md)).
- Unit tests run in Node with coverage floors for the rules, sound and core modules;
  browser checks run in Chromium in CI, including the production build at `/liano/`.
- The plan is [PLAN.md](../../PLAN.md), with the detail by area under
  [docs/plans/](../plans/).
